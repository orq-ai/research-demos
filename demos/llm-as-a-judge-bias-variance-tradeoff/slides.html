Bias & Variance
in LLM-as-a-Judge

Single judge, repeated judge, or panel? What the recent literature says about which strategy reduces which error — and why it matters for evaluation budgets.

Research · 2026-05-22 8 slides ~6 min
The problem

A single judge carries systematic biases

  • Position bias
    Order of candidates flips outcomes. Not random — varies by judge and task; modulated by quality gap (Shi et al. 2025).
  • Verbosity bias
    Longer answers preferred regardless of substance.
  • Self-preference
    Judges favor outputs from their own model family (Zheng 2023, Verga 2024).
  • Reasoning ceiling
    Judge inherits its own blind spots — math, logic, edge cases.

The key insight

Repetition ≠ bias correction

Adding more prompts or generations with the same judge shrinks sampling noise — not judge bias.

Zhu et al. (2026) show the judge-identity variance component is independent of sample count. To shrink it, you must change K — the number of distinct judges. This is the bias-variance tradeoff, stated cleanly for LLM evaluators.

Three panel strategies

Aggregation matters as much as diversity

  • PoLL — small diverse jury, majority vote
    Verga et al. 2024. Three judges from disjoint families beat single GPT-4 on 6 datasets at ~7× lower cost. Bias cancellation depends on family diversity.
  • BT-σ — reliability-weighted aggregation
    Qian et al. 2026. Bradley-Terry extended with per-judge discriminator, learned unsupervised. Beats naive averaging when judges differ in quality.
  • CyclicJudge — balanced round-robin
    Zhu et al. 2026. Each judge scores each cell at most once, equal usage overall. Cancels bias in expectation AND minimizes variance at fixed budget.

For our eval framework

How to spend the budget

  • Internal iteration → single strong judge + position swap
    Cheap. Sufficient for relative deltas under a held-fixed judge.
  • Leaderboards & cross-provider comparison → CyclicJudge round-robin
    ≥3 judges from disjoint families. Same call budget, ~30% lower variance, unbiased ranking.
  • Heterogeneous judge quality → BT-σ weighted aggregation
    When mixing frontier + small open-weight judges. Learns reliability unsupervised.
  • Always report the judge(s)
    Judge identity is now understood to be the dominant variance source. Pin versions.
